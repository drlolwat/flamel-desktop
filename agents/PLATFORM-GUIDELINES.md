# Current-platform content source policy

Static social-platform tactics are not authoritative. Platform limits, ranking behavior, community rules, moderation policy, formats, and commercial terms can change, so Flamel must verify current official documentation and applicable community rules when a task depends on them.

The governed `current-platform-content` skill owns platform adaptation. It requires a named platform, audience, source message, account voice, and moderation constraints; records source dates and uncertainty; and rejects spam, automation evasion, fabricated trends, guaranteed reach, or attempts to work around enforcement.

Historical platform-persona files remain audit sources only. They are not embedded personas and their frozen tactics must not guide a turn.
