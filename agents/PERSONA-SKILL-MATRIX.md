# Persona-to-skill disposition matrix

This document is the traceability record for the 42 former general-persona sources under agents/. README.md and PLATFORM-GUIDELINES.md are source documents, not personas, and are handled separately below.

## Classification contract

A workflow has one named owner skill even when several personas contribute material. Other skills may depend on that owner, but must not restate its trigger or checklist. Every governed skill must have:

- A bounded positive trigger and an explicit non-trigger.
- Declared inputs and required capabilities; absent data must be requested or reported, never invented.
- A concrete deliverable and item-scoped acceptance evidence.
- Provider-neutral instructions projected identically to Codex and Claude unless a capability is unavailable.
- Current primary-source verification for platform, legal, market, pricing, framework, or policy claims that can change.
- No roleplay, hype, fake benchmarks, mandatory delegation, unconditional automatic activation, hardcoded retired tool names, or fixed sprint cadence.
- Silent application of internal quality rubrics; user-facing output contains decisions, trade-offs, sources, and evidence.

Disposition values are:

- **Retain — thin delegation persona:** isolated domain context materially helps parallel or specialist work. The persona becomes a concise overlay and delegates its reusable workflow to the named governed skill.
- **Extract/merge — governed skill:** the reusable workflow belongs in the named skill; the personality wrapper is removed from normal routing.
- **Project-scope — governed skill:** useful only when the matching project explicitly installs it.
- **Retire/quarantine — no automatic projection:** redundant with native capabilities or unsafe/noisy as a default. Historical content remains available for rollback but does not guide turns.

Publication gates:

1. Compare the repository inventory with this table in both directions; fail on missing, extra, or duplicate persona names.
2. Verify every row has exactly one disposition and one workflow owner from the Single-owner skill taxonomy.
3. Verify every owner has a bounded trigger, required inputs, deliverable, and acceptance evidence.
4. Verify the four platform-specific personas resolve to current-platform-content and require live research.
5. Verify date-checker and file-creator resolve to native capabilities and studio-coach loses automatic/master-orchestrator behavior.
6. Verify retained persona overlays contain no provider tool names or reusable workflow duplication after conversion.
