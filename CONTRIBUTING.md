# Contributing

This is a source-available project under the Flamel Community License 1.0. Paid work and internal business use are allowed; monetizing Flamel itself requires separate permission. Read LICENSE.md for the terms.

Before accepting outside contributions, the maintainer must complete legal review of CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md, identify the accepting legal person, and configure recorded agreement acceptance. Contribution acceptance is currently disabled. Do not merge external contributions on the assumption that a pull request or sign-off supplies commercial relicensing rights.

The agreement is nonexclusive: contributors retain ownership while permitting Flamel to use and sublicense accepted contributions in its commercial products. Do not contribute private service code, secrets, or third-party material without compatible rights. Add meaningful tests for behavior and security boundaries affected by a contribution.

Forks may remain free competitors. Distribute modified binaries with their corresponding source and build instructions, rebrand them clearly, and use your own signing and update configuration. Voluntary donations must give no gated benefit.
