# Flamel Contributor Agreement 1.0 — proposed form

This form requires legal review and identification of the accepting legal person before contribution acceptance is enabled. A pull request, commit, or Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off alone is not acceptance of this agreement.

In the completed agreement, “Maintainer” is the named person or legal entity operating Flamel, identified privately in the acceptance record. Flamel.dev is the public project name and commercial inquiries go through https://flamel.dev; it is not a representation that a separate legal entity exists. Legal names and signatures must not be committed to the public repository. “Contribution” is the code, documentation, or other material explicitly identified by repository and commit hashes in that record.

1. You retain ownership of your Contribution. You grant the Maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to reproduce, modify, prepare derivative works of, distribute, publicly perform and display, sublicense, and commercially exploit your Contribution. This includes use in proprietary Flamel products and services and distribution under different licenses, without an obligation to publish private source or pay royalties.
2. You grant the Maintainer and recipients a perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive, royalty-free patent license to make, use, offer, sell, import, and otherwise transfer the Contribution and its combination with the Flamel project as submitted, to the extent your controlled patent claims necessarily cover that contribution or combination. No unrelated patent rights are granted.
3. You represent that you have authority to make these grants, have disclosed third-party material and its license, and have obtained any required employer authorization. Identify material excluded from these grants before acceptance. Do not submit confidential information, production secrets, or material you lack authority to contribute.
4. You provide the Contribution without warranties beyond the authority and disclosure statements above, to the extent law allows. You undertake no ongoing support duty. Nothing requires the Maintainer to accept or use it.
5. This agreement grants no trademark rights and does not transfer ownership. You may continue to use and license your Contribution independently. The Maintainer may assign these licenses with the Flamel project or business, and may sublicense them.

The acceptance record must identify the contributor's legal name, organization and signing authority where applicable, the Maintainer's legal name, agreement version and document hash, covered repositories/commits, date, and affirmative signature or equivalent recorded assent. Store legal identity records privately; public pull requests should show only verification status. Additional contributions require express coverage in the accepted agreement or a new record.
